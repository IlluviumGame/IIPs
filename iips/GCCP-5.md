## Summary:

This proposal recommends temporarily pausing the Illuvium Community Council (ICC) during the current period of reduced market activity and community participation, consolidating its responsibilities under the Illuvium Main Council (IMC). The objective is to improve governance efficiency, reduce operational overhead, and align DAO structures with present day participation levels, while preserving the option to reinstate the ICC when conditions improve.

## Overview:

The Illuvium DAO is currently operating in a prolonged period of reduced market activity. Crypto prices remain suppressed, on-chain participation is lower across the industry, and community engagement has slowed accordingly. Within this broader context, Web3 gaming has been hit particularly hard, with fewer active users, lower transaction volumes, and reduced governance participation compared to prior epochs.

Governance structures like the ICC were designed for periods of higher activity, frequent proposals, and sustained community engagement. Under current conditions, however, the level of participation and proposal throughput does not justify maintaining a separate community council layer. In practice, the ICC’s responsibilities can be handled by the IMC without compromising governance quality or decentralization.

This proposal does not question the value of the ICC in principle. Instead, it recognizes that DAO structures should scale with reality. Temporarily pausing the ICC allows the DAO to operate more efficiently during a low activity phase, reduce unnecessary overhead, and remain flexible. When market conditions, community participation, and governance demand recover, the ICC can be reinstated through a future proposal.

## Proposal:

**Pause of the Illuvium Community Council**
-The Illuvium Community Council (ICC) will be temporarily paused starting in Epoch 12.
-ICC members will cease active duties during the pause period.
-ICC salaries and operational expenses will be suspended for the duration of the pause.

**Transfer of Responsibilities to IMC**
-All governance, review, and community-facing responsibilities currently assigned to the ICC will be handled by the Illuvium Main Council (IMC).
-The IMC already possesses the mandate, context, and authority to perform these functions without requiring additional structural changes.

## Rationale:

This proposal is motivated by three core observations:

**1. Low Community Activity**
Proposal frequency, forum engagement, and participation metrics do not currently justify a separate community council layer.
**2. Market Conditions**
With ILV prices and treasury inflows under pressure, governance spending should scale proportionally to activity and impact.
**3. Operational Efficiency**
The IMC is capable of absorbing ICC responsibilities without reducing governance quality, responsiveness, or decentralization.

Pausing the ICC aligns DAO structure with current reality while maintaining flexibility for future expansion.

## Reinstatement Conditions:

The ICC may be reinstated through a future governance proposal if one or more of the following conditions are met:

-Sustained increase in governance participation.
-Meaningful rise in proposal volume requiring delegation.
-Improvement in market conditions supporting expanded governance spend.
-Clear community demand for a reinstated ICC.

## Implementation:

-The pause of the ICC will take effect at the start of the next governance epoch (12). 
-All ICC related compensation and operations will be suspended during the pause period.
-IMC will assume all ICC responsibilities immediately upon implementation (In Epoch 12)

## Conclusion:

This proposal reflects a pragmatic adjustment to current conditions, prioritizing efficiency, fiscal responsibility, and governance clarity. By temporarily pausing the ICC and consolidating responsibilities within the IMC, the DAO can maintain effective governance while preserving optionality for future expansion when activity and engagement justify it.
